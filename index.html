Wallpapers made for both screens of iPhone Duo.

Twofold is a free wallpaper app for iPhone Duo that saves matched cover and inner screen sets at exact pixel sizes, with no ads and no data collected.

Get it free on the App Store

Free. No ads, no account, no data collected. Works on any iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

What you get

  • 250 original wallpapers in 22 collections: photographic still life, monochrome, doodles, clay dioramas, pixel art and motivational quotes.
  • Exact sizes from Apple's specs: 1398 x 2034 for the cover display, and 2670 x 1878 wide or 1878 x 2670 upright for the inner display.
  • Preview the cover, the inner display or both, with a Lock Screen clock, before you save.
  • Dim and remix, a new pick every day, a shuffle button and offline favourites.
  • Saves to Photos with add-only access, then shows the three steps to set it.
