Privacy policy

DuoWallpaper collects no data.

  • No account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app makes no network requests. Wallpapers are drawn and stored on your device.
  • Photos: the app asks only for permission to add images, and only when you tap Save. It cannot read your library.
  • Favourites and settings are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Questions: open a support request.

Effective 2026-10-23.
